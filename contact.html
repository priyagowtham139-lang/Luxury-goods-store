Frequently asked

Quick answers to common questions

How do I book a Salem visit?

You can book directly by emailing hello@thestackly.com or calling 7010792745. Walk-ins are always welcomed during open hours, though appointments guarantee dedicated time with one of our master artisans.

Can I send an item for repair?

Yes. All Stackly creations are made for life. Send photographs and a brief description of the wear or repair needed to hello@thestackly.com. Our Salem workshop will evaluate it and provide complimentary shipping labels and repair estimates.

Are bespoke orders available year-round?

Bespoke commissions are accepted throughout the year based on artisan capacity. Typical lead time is 6 to 10 weeks depending on hide selection, hardware engraving, and custom measurements.

What is the return policy for online orders?

We offer complimentary returns within 30 days of receipt on all non-customized pieces in their original, unused condition with all packaging and authenticity certificates included.
