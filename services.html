How a bespoke piece happens

Four stages from first sketch to final sign-off.

  1. 1

    Consultation

    We discuss leather, hardware and how you will use the piece.

  2. 2

    Pattern & cut

    We build a bespoke paper pattern, cut the leather and send photos.

  3. 3

    Making

    Stitching by hand in Florence, typically three to four weeks.

  4. 4

    Delivery

    Insured, tracked and signed by the artisan on the inside seam.

Questions,
answered

How long does a bespoke commission take?

Most commissions are delivered in six to ten weeks, depending on leather curing and metal finishing.

Do you repair other brands?

Yes. We quote any heritage leather piece or mechanical watch free of charge within three working days.

Can I cancel a commission?

You can cancel before hide cutting begins for a full refund of your commission deposit.

Do you ship worldwide?

We ship fully insured to most countries; all import documentation and tracking are managed by our concierge.
