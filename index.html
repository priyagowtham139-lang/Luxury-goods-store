How long does a piece take?

Usually three weeks from choosing to delivery, with photos of your piece at every stage.

Can I change the finish after reserving?

Yes, until the artisan cuts the first panel. After that the pattern is fixed and signed.

What if an edition sells out?

Numbered editions do not return. Join the list and you will hear first about the next run.

Do you ship outside Italy?

We ship worldwide, insured and hand-wrapped, with tracking from the atelier door to yours.
